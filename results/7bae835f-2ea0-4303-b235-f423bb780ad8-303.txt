16 Drumboe.—The Church of Drumboe, with the chapel, was valued at three
marks in the Taxation of Pope Nicholas. Formerly the Bishop of Down had 12
earucates of land in Drumboe, as appears from an ancient document relating to the
property of the See. These were taxed in the King’s Books in October, 1616, at
60s. ; but it would seem that his claim at that date was only traditional, for an
Inquis. 17 Jac. I., found that ‘‘ the twelve towns of Drumboe are not, neither have
been, in the possession of the Bishop of Down within the memory of man. Jurors
say that the twelve towns are spiritual lands.” The Protestant Bishop (Eclin), in
1622, says :—‘* The XII. towne landes of Drombo, and the foure towne landes of
Blarys, let in fee farme by the late Bp. Dondasse unto Sir Hugh Montgomery and

Franciscan Friary ; a monastery was founded here for Fran-
ciscans, on the south side of the town, in the year 1224, by
Maurice Fitzgerald ;° who, it is said, erected this house on
the following occasion’: being about to build a castle in the
town, and the workmen who were digging the foundation, on
the eve of some festival, requesting a piece of money to drink
his health, he directed his eldest son to give it, who, instead
of obeying, abused the said workmen, at which Maurice was
so concerned, that he altered his design, and changed the
castle into an abbey. The founder was Lord Justice of Ire-
land in the years 1229 and 1232;? after which he retired to
this monastery, where he took on him the habit of St. Francis,
and dying the 8th of May, 1257, was interred here in the
habit of his order.4
